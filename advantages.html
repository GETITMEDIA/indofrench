TECHNOLOGIES WE ARE PROVIDING

Dunlop Technology

Indofrench mattresses feature the cutting-edge Dunlop technology, renowned for its ability to create exceptionally durable and supportive mattresses. Through a unique manufacturing process, Dunlop ensures consistent density across the entire mattress, resulting in optimal body support. This technology effectively reduces pressure points, relieving discomfort and promoting better spinal alignment. With Indofrench's implementation of Dunlop Technology, you can enjoy a truly restful sleep experience, waking up rejuvenated and ready to tackle the day.

Pincore Technology.

Indofrench mattresses are equipped with the innovative Pincore technology, which sets them apart from the rest. This technology incorporates evenly spaced pinholes throughout the mattress, facilitating superior air circulation. The pinholes promote enhanced breathability, allowing for efficient heat dissipation and moisture evaporation. As a result, you can enjoy a consistently cool and comfortable sleep environment, ensuring a refreshing and uninterrupted night's rest. Indofrench's integration of Pincore Technology guarantees an optimal sleep climate for a truly rejuvenating experience.

Three Time Purification Technology.

In addition to advanced technologies, Indofrench mattresses incorporate a unique Three-Time Purification (TTP) technology. This technology removes allergens, dust mites, and other microscopic impurities that can accumulate over time. By providing a cleaner sleep surface, this purification process contributes to a healthier sleep environment, particularly for individuals with allergies or respiratory sensitivities.

HEALTH AND WELL-BEING.

Optimal Spinal Alignment.

Indofrench mattresses are carefully engineered with your spinal health in mind. By utilizing advanced technologies such as Dunlop and Pincore, these mattresses provide optimal spinal alignment while you sleep. The Dunlop technology ensures consistent density throughout the mattress, offering optimal support for your body. Combined with Pincore technology, which enhances air circulation, these mattresses alleviate pressure points and contribute to better spinal alignment. This helps reduce back and neck pain, minimize the risk of sleep-related issues, and ultimately improves your overall sleep quality.

Restorative Sleep.

Indofrench recognizes the vital role of quality sleep in rejuvenating both your body and mind. Our mattresses are designed to promote deep and restorative sleep, enabling you to wake up feeling refreshed and energized. Indofrench mattresses minimize disturbances and provide superior comfort, allowing you to achieve a deeper sleep state. By supporting uninterrupted and restful sleep, these mattresses enhance your overall well-being, improving cognitive function, mood, and overall productivity throughout the day.

Dust Free Mattress.

Creating a safe and healthy sleep environment is a priority when choosing a mattress. Indofrench takes this aspect seriously and ensures our mattresses are free from carcinogenic chemicals commonly found in conventional mattresses. Indofrench utilizes high-quality materials that are free from harmful chemicals, ensuring a safer sleeping experience. Many traditional mattresses contain chemicals such as formaldehyde, flame retardants, and volatile organic compounds (VOCs), which can be detrimental to your health. By avoiding the use of these chemicals, Indofrench mattresses provide a healthier alternative, reducing the risk of allergic reactions and respiratory issues.
