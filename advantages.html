PROPRIETARY TECHNOLOGIES WE PROVIDE

Dunlop Technology

Indofrench mattresses feature cutting-edge Dunlop technology, renowned for creating exceptionally durable and supportive mattresses. Through a unique manufacturing process, Dunlop ensures consistent density across the entire mattress, resulting in optimal body support. This technology effectively reduces pressure points, relieving discomfort and promoting better spinal alignment. With Dunlop Technology, you wake up rejuvenated and ready for the day.

Pincore Technology

Indofrench mattresses are equipped with innovative Pincore technology featuring 3000+ evenly spaced pinholes throughout the mattress core. The pinholes promote enhanced breathability, allowing for efficient heat dissipation and moisture evaporation. As a result, you enjoy a consistently cool and comfortable sleep environment in all Indian climate seasons.

Three-Time Purification (TTP)

In addition to advanced core molds, Indofrench mattresses incorporate a unique Three-Time Purification (TTP) process. This multi-stage washing removes rubber proteins, allergens, dust mites, and microscopic impurities. By providing a clean sleep surface, TTP contributes to a healthier sleep environment, particularly for individuals with asthma or allergies.

HEALTH AND WELL-BEING BENEFITS

Optimal Spinal Alignment

Indofrench mattresses are engineered with your spinal health in mind. By combining Dunlop density with 7-zone pincore support, these mattresses keep your spine in a natural ergonomic alignment while sleeping. This alleviates lower back pressure, reduces neck strain, and prevents morning stiffness.

Restorative Deep Sleep

Quality sleep is essential for restoring both body and mind. Indofrench mattresses minimize toss-and-turn disturbances with superior motion isolation, enabling you to achieve deeper REM sleep states. You wake up energized, with improved mood, focus, and productivity throughout the day.

Dust-Free & Chemical-Free

Conventional synthetic foam mattresses often contain harmful VOCs, formaldehyde, and chemical flame retardants. Indofrench utilizes 100% organic rubber sap with zero carcinogenic chemicals, providing a completely safe, non-toxic, and hypoallergenic sleep surface for your family.
