FAQS

General FAQS

What is the density?
The density varies according to the size and thickness. However we have soft, medium, hard and extra hard.
Is it 100% natural rubber latex?
Yes, Indofrench mattresses are manufactured using 100% pure organic rubber latex without any synthetic fillers, PU foam, or toxic chemicals.
Where is the latex sourced from?
Our natural rubber sap is harvested directly from sustainable rubber tree plantations in Kerala and South India.
Are the prices same in all stores in Kochi?
Yes, uniform factory-recommended standard pricing applies across all authorized Indofrench showrooms and dealer partners.
What is Dunlop Technology?
Dunlop process involves liquid rubber sap whipped into foam, poured into pincore molds, and steam vulcanized to create a durable, breathable 7-zone supportive core.
Does it have a zip cover?
Yes, all Indofrench mattresses come with a removable, washable organic cotton zippered inner and outer protective cover.
What is the weight in Kg?
The weight depends on mattress dimensions and thickness, ranging from approximately 18 kg for single size up to 50+ kg for king size mattresses.
Are two latex pillows free with the mattress?
Yes, complimentary 100% natural latex pillows are included with selected queen and king size mattress purchases.
What is the maximum weight of persons who can sleep on it?
Indofrench mattresses are engineered for high resilience and can comfortably support individuals weighing up to 150 kg+ without permanent sagging.
Can two persons of 75 kg weight sleep?
Yes, absolutely. The open pincore structure provides point elasticity and motion isolation for both sleepers.
How many years guarantee?
Indofrench offers an unmatched 10-Year full piece-to-piece replacement guarantee against manufacturing or indentation defects.
If I buy in Kochi, will the guarantee be valid at Chennai if I shift there?
Yes, your 10-Year guarantee is registered online and valid across all cities and states in India.
How long can it be used- maximum life?
Because natural rubber does not degrade like synthetic foam, an Indofrench natural latex mattress lasts between 15 to 25 years with optimal comfort.
