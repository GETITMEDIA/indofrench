Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about 100% natural latex mattresses, Dunlop pincore process, density options, and our 10-year warranty guarantee.

01 What is the density of Indofrench mattresses?
The density varies according to mattress size, thickness, and firm specifications. We offer customized firmness options including Soft, Medium, Hard, and Extra Hard to match your personal spinal alignment preferences.
02 Is it manufactured using 100% natural rubber latex?
Yes! Indofrench mattresses are manufactured using 100% pure organic natural rubber latex without any synthetic petroleum fillers, polyurethane (PU) memory foam, or toxic chemicals.
03 Where is the natural latex sap sourced from?
Our high-purity natural rubber latex sap is sustainably harvested directly from eco-friendly rubber tree plantations in Kerala and South India, processed at our Puducherry factory unit since 1979.
04 Are prices the same across all stores in Kochi and India?
Yes, uniform factory-recommended standard pricing applies across all authorized Indofrench brand showrooms, dealer partners, and online orders pan-India.
05 What is Dunlop process technology?
The Dunlop manufacturing process involves whipping pure liquid latex sap into a smooth foam, pouring it into specialized 3000+ pincore molds, and steam vulcanizing it to create a dense, breathable, and supportive core.
06 Does the mattress include a zippered protective cover?
Yes! All Indofrench mattresses feature a premium, removable, and washable organic cotton zippered inner and outer protective fabric cover for complete hygiene.
07 What is the weight of an Indofrench mattress in Kg?
The weight depends on dimension and thickness. Single size mattresses weigh around 18 - 25 kg, while Queen and King size natural latex mattresses range from 35 kg to 55+ kg due to high rubber density.
08 Are complimentary latex pillows included with the mattress?
Yes, complimentary 100% natural latex contour pillows are included with selected Queen and King size mattress purchases under our ongoing seasonal promotion offers!
09 What is the maximum weight capacity supported per person?
Indofrench natural latex mattresses are engineered for extreme resilience and can comfortably support sleeper weights up to 150+ kg per person without permanent indentation or sagging.
10 Can two persons weighing 75+ kg sleep comfortably?
Yes, absolutely. The open-cell pincore structure offers zero motion transfer, point elasticity, and ideal spinal alignment for both partners sleeping together.
11 How many years of warranty / guarantee is provided?
Indofrench offers an industry-leading 10-Year piece-to-piece replacement guarantee against manufacturing defect or sagging greater than 1 inch.
12 Is my warranty valid if I relocate to another city like Chennai?
Yes! Your 10-Year guarantee is digitally registered online on our website system and remains 100% valid across all cities, dealers, and states in India.
13 What is the total expected lifespan of a natural latex mattress?
Because 100% natural rubber latex does not decompose or flatten like synthetic foam, an Indofrench mattress lasts between 15 to 25 years while maintaining optimal elasticity.
